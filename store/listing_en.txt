[Description]  (max 10,000 characters)
Most PDF tools "edit" text by covering it with a white box and typing on top. Revora PDF changes the text itself.

Click any line of text and type. Revora finds the font the PDF uses among the fonts installed in Windows and writes your change with the same font, size, weight, color and transparency, so the result looks like the original document.

EDIT TEXT
- Click to edit, drag to move, rotate, change opacity
- Multi-line text with adjustable line spacing
- Add new text anywhere on the page with a live preview
- Delete a single piece of text without touching what is above or below it

LINES AND TABLES
- Select table lines as real objects: move, resize, bend, recolor, make them dashed
- Box-select a whole table grid and move it at once
- New lines and boxes snap to existing corners

MARKUP THAT STAYS EDITABLE
- Arrows, boxes, circles and notes with shadows and outlines
- Pen and highlighter; the highlighter snaps to words and creates real PDF highlights
- Numbered badges, magnifier/detail view, eraser and your own note templates
- Signatures: draw once, or load a photo of your paper signature (the background is removed)
- Add images, logos and stamps, with rotation
- Blur that really removes the text underneath (redaction), instead of just covering it
- Everything can be selected and changed again after saving, and shows correctly in other PDF readers

PAGES AND DOCUMENTS
- Reorder pages by dragging thumbnails; rotate, delete, duplicate, insert blank pages
- Insert pages from another PDF, merge and split PDFs
- Watermarks and form filling
- Images to PDF (keeps JPEG quality and fixes phone photo orientation)
- Export pages as PNG/JPG up to 600 dpi; extract embedded photos at full resolution
- OCR for scanned pages (requires the free Tesseract OCR engine)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no subscription, no cloud. Revora works completely offline and never uploads your documents.

Try it free for 7 days, then buy once and keep it - no subscription.

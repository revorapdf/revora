Everything you need for everyday PDFs

Edit, mark up, sign and organize, in one fast desktop app.

Edit text in place

Click a line and type. Move, rotate and delete text without touching what is around it. Multi-line text with line spacing, and new text anywhere on the page.

Tables and lines as real objects

Select table lines and move, resize, bend or restyle them. Box-select a whole grid and move it at once; new lines snap to corners.

Markup that stays editable

Arrows, notes, highlights that snap to words, numbered badges, a magnifier for details, signatures and stamps. Everything can be changed again after saving.
